help_outline How to download your Instagram ZIP
  1. Open Instagram, go to your profile and tap the hamburger menu.
  2. Tap Account Centre.
  3. Go to Your information and permissions.
  4. Select Download your information / Export your information.
  5. Choose Create export → To device.
  6. In Customise information, select Connections.
  7. Set Date range to All time.
  8. Set Format to JSON.
  9. Set Media quality to Low (faster export, smaller file).
  10. Tap Start export.

After some time, Instagram will email you when your export is ready. Download the ZIP from that email and upload it here.
